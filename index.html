The Problem — SIH 2026 PS #26100

Manual Bid Verification Is Slow, Error‑Prone & Exploitable

Every GeM tender today is cross-checked by procurement officers reading through hundreds of pages of EMD certificates, Udyam registrations, and technical/financial bids — clause by clause, by hand.

Delays at Scale

GeM handles a very high daily volume of bids across goods, services & works. Manually cross-checking EMD, Udyam and technical annexures for each one takes officers hours per bid, creating evaluation backlogs.

Human Error

A single missed clause — an expired Udyam certificate, an unsigned affidavit, a mismatched UDIN — causes wrongful technical rejections, or worse, lets a non-compliant bid through evaluation.

Fraud Slips Through

Duplicate PAN/GSTIN across shell vendors, reused EMD bank-guarantee UTRs, and forged signatures are nearly impossible to catch by eye across thousands of concurrent tenders.

Our Outcome

BidSeva is designed to reduce repetitive document cross-checking while keeping the procurement officer in control. Pilot targets: ≥95% clause-match precision and measurable reduction in review time.

The 7 Stages of Public Procurement in India

Understanding how Indian government departments structure tenders from initial requirement to contract execution.

01

Requirement Identification

Departments identify operational needs, budget allocations, and project scopes for infrastructure, goods, or services.

02

Tender Document Prep

Drafting technical specs, qualification criteria, EMD terms, payment conditions, and NIT (Notice Inviting Tender) details.

03

Tender Publication

Official publishing on portals such as CPPP, GeM, state eProcurement portals, and national newspapers.

04

Pre-Bid Queries & Corrigenda

Pre-bid meetings where vendors submit clarifications, leading to official corrigenda or addenda updates.

05

Submission of Bids

Bidders upload digital signatures, EMD proofs, technical proposals, and financial quotes (BOQs) before deadlines.

06

Evaluation & Comparison

Two-cover evaluation: Technical bid verification followed by financial opening to identify the lowest valid L1 bidder.

Award of Contract (LoA)

Issuance of Letter of Award (LoA), submission of Performance Bank Guarantee (PBG), and official contract execution.

How BidSeva Works Under the Hood

A five-stage automated pipeline that takes raw tender & vendor documents from upload to an auditable officer decision.

1. Ingestion

RFP & vendor bundle upload (PDF/DOCX/ZIP), plus GeM Bid Search API pull

2. OCR Extraction

OCR engine pulls text, tables & signatures from scanned pages

3. NLP Clause Matching

Clause extraction model maps vendor fields to RFP eligibility criteria

4. Fraud & Risk Scoring

Rules engine + anomaly model checks PAN/GSTIN duplication, UTR reuse, metadata tampering

5. Officer Dashboard

Ranked bidders, explainable flags & audit log surfaced for human approval
